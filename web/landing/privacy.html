Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

WARDEN collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier and no server-side profile. Nothing you browse is transmitted to us, because there is no service to transmit it to.

What the app stores

  • Desktop (Windows): settings, bookmarks and history live in %APPDATA%\WARDEN on your machine. Cookies are memory-only and gone when the window closes. Uninstalling leaves that folder in place unless you remove it.
  • Mobile: only the app's own preferences. There is no browsing history, no cookie jar and no shared session; pages open in the platform's private browser surface.

Network connections

Pages you visit load from their own servers, as in any browser, and those sites have their own policies. WARDEN adds three connections of its own, all optional and documented in the repository: release metadata from GitHub when checking for updates (desktop), DNS-over-HTTPS to the resolver you select, and any proxy or tunnel endpoint you configure. None of them carries usage data.

Camera, microphone and location

The mobile app never requests camera, microphone or location. The desktop app refuses every permission except camera and microphone, and grants those only to a site you explicitly allowlisted; even then the page receives a locally processed track and the raw device stream never leaves the app.

Children

The app is a general-purpose tool, not directed at children, and collects no personal information from anyone.
